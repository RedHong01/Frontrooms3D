# Sources · doors (交互设计)

This folder's sources live in two numbered lists:
- **1–127**: the 2026-10-31 contact-door research, numbered at the end of `10_research.md`.
- **R1–R50** (below): the 2026-11-04 research on Resident Evil Requiem's doors, for Red's ask in the 交互设计 chat (11-04): "我希望你能够重点研究最新的生化危机游戏安魂曲里的门的交互是怎么做的，然后优化现有的门的交互。" They are cited as [R#] in `10_research.md` (block "Resident Evil Requiem (2026)" and the RE notes beside it) and in `20_design.md` §2b.

How they were read (2026-11-04):
- A 交互设计 research agent used web search and page reads. Nothing was downloaded, no account was used, and no video was watched, so no video timestamp is given anywhere.
- **fetched** = the page text was read. **snippet** = only a search-result summary was seen (the page was blocked or gone).
- 交互设计 re-read R1 and R39 itself on the same day.
- Coverage notes are paraphrases. The only quote kept is one line of R1, in `10_research.md`.
- The agent's raw notes (claims tagged VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN, a search log) were in the session scratchpad and are not kept in the repo. Everything they support is in `10_research.md`.

**What these sources can carry.** No written source describes Requiem's doors in depth. There are no timings, no description of the door animation, and nothing on door sound or door haptics. The strong points:
- one post-launch tips guide (R1) for closing a door and peeking through a crack;
- four write-ups of the same Summer Game Fest 2025 demo beat (R2–R5) for closing a door on the stalker;
- walkthroughs (R8, R9) for the scripted door break;
- PlayStation.Blog (R6, R7) for Leon's door verb and Capcom's DualSense list.

R19 has the most door detail, but it reads as house or machine-written content and two sister pages now return 404. It is a lead only.
