**Status: v1.** It answers 游戏视觉's critic item E15 (`50_critic_fixes.md`): the props' palette was marked ESTIMATE, and "a catalogue reading, 1988–91" was asked of 叙事.
- **Evidence:**
  - *Contract* magazine 1985–93 (archive.org US Modernist scans, full text searched and read around each hit);
  - a 1984–85 office-furniture dealer catalogue and a 1993 distributor catalogue;
  - a dated 1990 newspaper ad;
  - a 1989 Boston building specification;
  - 1988–90 colour-trend articles.
- **Sources:** `E`-numbers (`SOURCES.md` §E). Full notes: `~/FrontRoomsNarrativeWork/station/D_palette.md`. Nothing was downloaded; brand names are research only.
- **Scope: props and furniture only.** The walls are decided: Red's walls 2 · Post Room, 15:4x, 游戏视觉 chat (blue-grey eggshell #9AA3A6, a navy-charcoal crash rail, VCT #B2AEA3, dark-bronze frames).
- **Weak spot:** fire-alarm enclosures. No 1985–93 catalogue page stating a colour was found; that row rests on a 1989 specification plus current reseller pages.

## How the verdicts meet our rules (叙事's reading)

- **Annunciator: choose the beige cabinet with the beige door.** The red options are period-true, but red already means the ALARM ZONE card in our game (RELAY §6), and the critics wanted the panel to recede beside the key (E1). A beige cabinet keeps it matte and quiet, and nothing period says "putty". The lamps stay dark except the green POWER pilot (`01` §2).
- **Pull stations** are red in every source. None are in the kit. If one is ever added, keep it out of the take frame, so no red object competes with the key.
- **Beam seating in slate blue or charcoal suits the Post Room walls** (blue-grey paint, a navy-charcoal rail). It also keeps B away from A24's upholstered showroom armchairs (`02_refs.md` §0). Navy stays possible, as a variant.
- **"Grey with mauve" was already passé by 1993** in the trade press (E06–E08). A grey task chair with one burgundy or teal accent reads as a 1990 office that was fitted out a few years earlier, which suits an after-hours post.
- **The guard desk variant** (putty steel pedestals, oak-grain top) is the archetypal 1980s institutional desk. It is a good fit for A, the Office post.

## Suggested swatch set (synthesis, not measured values; 游戏视觉 sets the numbers)

- **Beam seating:**
  - chrome or black beam, with slate-blue or charcoal vinyl seats;
  - one tired legacy bench with beige fibreglass shells and brown pads.
- **Reception counter:** medium-oak veneer fascia, grey laminate top.
- **Guard desk:** oak-grain or grey laminate. Variant: putty steel pedestals with an oak-grain top.
- **Task chair:** grey fabric on a black base.
- **Guest chairs:** chrome sled base, burgundy or teal seats.
- **Steel:** putty key cabinet and files.
- **Fire alarm:** beige cabinet with a beige door; green POWER pilot only.
- **Plastics:** putty or beige CRT and accessories.
