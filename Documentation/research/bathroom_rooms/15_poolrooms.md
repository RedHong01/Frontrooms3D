## 0. Short answer

1. **The look is one artist's before it is the Backrooms'.** The Poolrooms began as Jared Pike's *Dream Pools*, 3D renders posted on Instagram from late 2020 (Blender, LuxCore). Fans folded them into the Backrooms as Level 37 [PR3, PR4, PR5]. Pike's images, and the CC BY-SA images by u/AnarkyMusic on the Wikidot page, are the compositions we must never copy.
2. **The canon is short and specific** [PR1, PR2]: interconnected rooms and corridors under undulating, lukewarm, blue-green water; white ceramic tile on most surfaces, pristine and identical; areas too large for any pool and joined without sense; pillars standing in the water in impractical numbers; open water with no ledge to climb onto; light from irregular angles with some areas fully dark; staircases running straight down into deep, dark water; a small constant ripple on undisturbed water; sound that drops off, does not reverberate, and mutes speech; no entities.
3. **Pike's own recipe** [PR4, PR6]: an unclear light source, tiles too pristine and uniform, a daunting scale, proportions that do not fit a body, and darkness kept at the edges on purpose ("I try to lean into that and purposefully create dark areas"). Never show what is there. His fear of machinery behind underwater grates feeds the work.
4. **Why it works** [PR6, PR7]: a pool is built for bodies, noise and daylight. Take only the people away and the signals stay with nothing to complete them: chlorine and cold tile remembered from childhood, plus silence and dark corners. Calm and dread arrive together, which is why reactions split between serene and frightening [PR3].
5. **Almost every trait has a 1990 device** (§4): white glazed tile, wet-niche lamps, indirect metal halide, dead lamps that nobody relamped, recessed steps, main-drain and return grates, a sump stair. The pool level can be built from period parts without borrowing an image.
6. **Five traits collide with what we decided** (§5): canon white-only tile against D8's period palette; arches and curved forms (common in the images, not required by the canon text) against our "rectangles on the grid" rule; stairs into deep water against D6 (wading only); "devoid of entities" against D3 (the Relay wades in too); dampened sound against a real tiled hall's long tail. Each is either a variation in the direction stills (§6) or a recommendation.
7. **The strongest FrontRooms move is the gradient (D1 c):** the club is worn, coloured, signed and purposeful; past the far door the wear, colour, signs and purpose drain away until only white tile, water and badly placed light remain.

## 2. The canon, trait by trait (PR1, PR2)

| Trait | Canon wording (paraphrased unless quoted) |
|---|---|
| Plan | "an expansive complex of interconnected rooms and corridors submerged in undulating, lukewarm water"; areas range from uniform pools and hallways to open, abnormally shaped areas |
| Surface | most surfaces white ceramic tile; the only other colour is the blue-green water; tiles "eerily pristine", identical, undamaged (and practically indestructible) |
| Purpose | areas connect "in a senseless manner", none with an identifiable purpose; "much too large to properly serve the function of a pool" |
| Things in the water | pillars "much too large in quantity to be practical"; open water with no ledge to hoist onto |
| Light | "cast from irregular angles, leaving some areas completely cast in darkness"; the journal also calls the rooms "brightly-lit", tile reflecting light into every corner |
| Depth | staircases descending directly into deep pits of water; underwater holes; deep areas dark and without exits (Fandom adds currents) |
| Water | a minimal constant ripple on undisturbed water; Epsom salt; relaxing |
| Sound | noises drop off suddenly, sound does not reverberate as it should, speech feels muted; the water's sound calm, or rarely so loud it drowns everything |
| Life | devoid of entities; no other wanderers met |
| Feeling | "a calming sensation"; peace, then dread at "the strange, lightless corners"; "far too familiar, but far too strange to be comforting" |
| Joins | in through water, porcelain-tiled areas and an arcade; out through water, dark tunnels and corridors to similar corridors (01_ip_research §6 adds Level 4 via long dry corridors) |

## 3. Origin, and the guard it sets

- *Dream Pools*, Jared Pike, from late 2020: his first pool image drew little notice; the second, a week later, spread [PR4]. Blender with LuxCore [PR5].
- Pike designs against real pools on purpose: dark areas real pools do not have, tiles too uniform and pristine, clear blue-green water, proportions that do not fit a body [PR3, PR6].
- Fans named them the Poolrooms and made them Level 37; Fandom's images are Pike's, used with permission; Wikidot's are u/AnarkyMusic's under CC BY-SA 4.0 [PR1, PR2].
- **Guard (binding, kickoff "never copy a specific creator's level or images"):** no composition, camera angle, colour scheme or room layout taken from *Dream Pools*, u/AnarkyMusic's images, POOLS, Escape the Backrooms, Inside the Backrooms, Anemoiapolis, Kane Parsons' work or the two 2026 films. We work from the canon's words and from dated 1990 hardware; the downloaded game frames stay study material.

## 5. Where the canon meets what Red already decided

| Collision | Canon / images | Ours today | How to settle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colour | white tile only, blue-green water | D8: period palette (mint or teal band, quarry deck) | Use both: the club keeps the period palette, the level drains to white. Stills show the drain (S5, S6) |
| Arches and curves | common in Pike's images; the canon text asks only for "abnormally shaped areas" | 10_research §1.7 and zone_lamps §6: rectangles on the grid, no arches | Red's call from images: one S6 variant with round-headed doorways and bullnosed corners, one strictly rectangular. Arched openings fit the grid as door modules |
| Deep water | stairs into deep pits; swimming in POOLS | D6: wading only (1.07–1.37 m) | Keep D6: the stair runs down to a gate under the water line; darkness, not depth, says "deeper" |
| Entities | "devoid of entities", safe | D3: the Relay wades under the same law | Recommend keeping D3: the one level the canon calls safe is not, and 10_research §3.6 shows water as a waiting room fails |
| Sound | dampened, no reverberation | P6 asks a 2.5–4 s pool tail | Both: the club hall rings; the level is wrong because it does not. An ask for 声音设计 at the sound stage |
