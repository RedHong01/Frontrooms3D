## 0. Short answer

1. **The structural thesis:** the Poolrooms are a 1980s leisure pool and the basement of a 1990 office tower, assembled wrongly and repeated. Almost every structural trait of the canon has a dated counterpart in that building: tiled channels and beach entries (leisure pools, from Europe in the 1980s), windows into lit water (natatorium observation windows and service tunnels), stairs into water (recessed pool stairs, the tower's fire stair going down to a flooded sump), arches and round windows (1980s postmodern fit-outs), sourceless glow (glass block, back in fashion from 1983), pillars (the tower's column grid), oversized white boxes (racquetball courts turned into mirrored aerobics rooms) [PS1–PS9].
2. **So the level is not "white boxes with water" but the club's own building going wrong**, move by move (§2). The player recognises each piece from the club before it stops making sense.
3. **It also settles two collisions from 15 §5:** arches come from the club's 1980s postmodern fit-out, not from anyone's renders; "deeper" is a dark zone reached by the fire stair, at wading depth (D6), not deep water.
4. **Red's dark zone (F3) has a place:** the flooded lower basement at the foot of the fire stair, lit only by wet-niche lamps and the glow of observation windows.

## 3. The dark zone (Red's F3 pick)

- Where: the flooded lower basement at the foot of the fire stair (M3) and at the end of the service gallery (M4).
- Light: no overheads (troffers dead, M1); wet-niche lamps in the walls, observation windows glowing turquoise, ceiling caustics; ambient near-black teal (16 §3, "night").
- Water: still wading depth (D6); dark tile and absorption make it read deeper.
- Rules: the Relay wades here too (D3); darkness is never cover (rule 12), so the windows and lamps must keep the Relay readable.

## 4. Fit with the map and the owners

- Every move is a module on the 3 m grid: arched door, tiled column, suspended-ceiling bay (with dead or missing tiles), stair down to a gate, gallery with windows, glass-block wall, beach-entry floor, weir step, slide mouth. Contract diffs go to 关卡设计 (map files), the modules and materials to 游戏视觉 (kit, shader for the windows' underwater view).
- Sensory honesty: the aerobics room's mirrors would have to show the player and the Relay (D7); until a mirror path exists, the court is seen through clear glass instead.
- Era: everything here existed by 1990 (postmodern fit-outs, glass block, leisure pools, observation windows, suspended ceilings).
