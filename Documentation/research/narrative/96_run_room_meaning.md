# 96 · Run!: what it means, the honest door, the recede and the red (叙事设计, 2026-11-04)

Answers 叙事设计 cards 1-3 of `research/level_space/71_run_room.md` §9 and peer question P1 (briefs/narrative.md, 总控 11-03 23:07). All rest on Red's "全部按推荐" of 2026-11-03 23:18 (`71` §0.1). **No new player-visible string**, so nothing goes to the tables.

## 1. The frame and the message (card 1; decision 8a): signed

- **Run! is a fire compartment already on the alarm** (EGRESS, `relay_pursuit/30_narrative.md` §1). Stepping in trips an initiating device. The building does what an honest building does: it puts the compartment on emergency power and lights the way to its one door.
- **The message:** room one's first rule, `WALK, DO NOT RUN.`, fails here. Its second, `CLOSE DOORS BEHIND YOU.`, saves you. Nothing says RUN. The lights say it, and the rule against RUN as advice stays (`40` header).
- **The placard is not hung again inside the corridor.** That was my call to make (`71` §7.1).
  - The plan is the game's one honest teacher. The tutorial rests on "the plan is true" (`40` §3 A35: a lie on the second floor would undo LEVEL 0's lesson).
  - A placard posted where obeying it is the riskier choice turns the plan into a trap, and the player learns to distrust every plan after it.
  - The irony works without it: room one taught both rules minutes earlier, and the player brings them in. The ALARM ZONE card at the entrance is the room's own sign, and it tells the truth.
  - Cost saved: one prop. The flaw `71` names (players who obey and walk) also goes.

## 2. The deception is D1 (card 2; decision 8c): signed

- **An honest Run door that fools a habit.** Its square lite, its tell (the ALARM ZONE card and the red-banded detector base) and its light all tell the truth. What deceives is the player's hope and their habit of barging doors. `40` §1, "the building is honest about itself", holds. No new string.
- **D4, the changed door, is not built.** "The building lying" stays a deep-floor idea (`10` L0-7). If it is ever built, the fiction already holds an excuse the building gives in its own voice, so it never has to lie outright:
  - the plan's `THIS FLOOR MAY DIFFER FROM PLAN`;
  - R.M.'s memo, "Remodeling continues on several floors. Until it is finished, a floor may differ from its posted plan." (`70` §6, terminal set 1).
  - D4 would still need Red and this line to sign it.

## 4. A siren, or none (with 声音设计 4): none

- **No siren, no bell.** EGRESS's building-wide alarm is silent (`relay_pursuit/30_narrative.md` §6 rule 3: "No bell"). The station's key plate says `SILENT ALARM: REMOVAL SIGNALS PANEL`. A siren in Run! would make one room loud in a building that is silent everywhere else.
- Only the devices' own local sounds play, where they are (C07). The light carries the alarm.
- **At the trip (`71` §7.5 S4):** the door-holder thunk **without** the damped chime, or a device sound of the trip's own. **Never the chime.** It is the game's one "it sees you" sound, and at a trip nothing has seen the player.
- The red tube's ballast hum (声音设计 2, if Red says yes to the tube) is the tube's own sound, not an alarm. It is fine.

## 5. P1: may the fire-door frame behind the runner close by itself at the trip? No

- **Building-true but wrong here.** On alarm, real 1990 door holders release held-open fire doors (`30_narrative.md` §7, verified). But a door shutting behind the runner is `71`'s D3, "the door trips it, or shuts behind you", marked **never**. It also cuts F2, the step back.
- The frames behind stay open, like the ones ahead (`71` §4.8 item 8). Only the goal end's hold-open magnets let go (§5.7), and that is the door the player is running to shut.
- The map can shut a door in code (关卡设计's note), so this is a design call, and the answer is no.
