### 3.3 What decided the fix

- **The diffuser transmits the sRGB red primary.** Its emission is `(3.2, .013, .006)` linear × the lamp field: hue 0.1° before the grade.
- **The grade sets a floor near 6.5°.** Ladder of that red through the game's grade (bloom, grain, CA, lens off), linear input → sRGB: 0.2 → (100, 30, 18) 9.2°; 0.4 → (140, 36, 23) 6.8°; 0.6 → (170, 44, 28) 6.5°; 1.0 → (208, 60, 38) 7.7°; 2.0 → (247, 98, 60) 12.4°; 3.2 → (255, 130, 79) 17.4°; 10 → (255, 201, 146) 30.2°. Below 0.3 the warm lifted black (lift (1, .99, .94, .035), white balance +9 / −7) pulls hue up; above 1 the ACES shoulder does. Full table: `fix1/data/ladder_hdr_vs_ldr.csv`.
- **In a dark room the letters already sit at the floor.** Gain sweep, dark Lobby 3 m: 0.5 → 7.8°, 0.7 → 7.2°, 1 → 7.2°, 1.4 → 8.0°, 2 → 9.7° (hot spots 10 → 27°). Gain 1 kept.
- **In a lit room the emission was not the problem.** Lit Lobby 3 m: gain 1 → 13.6° / 0.54; gain 0.5 → 14.8° / 0.51; emission off → 25.6° / 0.39. So exposure- or ambient-aware scaling would make it worse: what desaturated the letters was the light the diffuser gave back. Decomposition at 3 m (raw letter RGB): emission (1.06, .006, .004); room light added (0.28, 0.15, 0.12), of which (0.13, 0.13, 0.11) is white specular (albedo 0 keeps it, smoothness 0 removes most of it).

### 3.4 Finding: the old capture path clipped at 1.0 (VL268)

- URP 17 (`UniversalRenderPipelineCore.CreateRenderTextureDescriptor`) uses a camera's target texture as its intermediate colour buffer ("External texture replaces internal (intermediate) color buffer"). An ARGB32 target therefore clamps the scene at 1.0 before bloom (threshold 1.05) and ACES. Same ladder through ARGB32: 3.2 → (208, 65, 41) 8.5°, 10 → (209, 78, 49) 11.0° (HDR: 17.4° and 30.2°); grey ≥ 1 plateaus at (210, 208, 196).
- Affected: `FrontRoomsExitSignLookdev` (build T2/T3 frames, es_b01/es_b02), `FrontRoomsExitSignStreamCapture` (integrate es_i0x), `FrontRoomsExitSignABCapture` (Red's calls), run 1 of this stage, and main's `FrontRoomsKitLookdev` (and its clone copy `FrontRoomsKitLookdevAccess`). Their graded frames understate bloom and the ACES shoulder on anything above 1.0. Their raw (ARGBFloat/Half, post off) numbers are unaffected.
- Fix for any harness: render into `RenderTextureFormat.ARGBHalf`, read back `RGBAHalf` (linear, display-referred: URP does no sRGB conversion for a texture target) and encode sRGB (`fix1/code/FrontRoomsExitSignFix1Capture.cs.txt`, `SaveGraded`).
