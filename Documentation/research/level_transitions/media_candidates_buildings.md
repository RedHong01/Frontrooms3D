# Media candidates · real buildings (for `02_real_buildings.md`)

Date: 2026-10-03. **Nothing here has been downloaded.** Red approves the batch; the visual chat asks once.
What is already on disk and used: 12 pages from *Interiors* June and October 1990 (`Research/week02/furniture-ads/magazines/`, approved 2026-10-02) → `images/rb_01`–`rb_12`; the A24 trailer frame `Research/week02/kit-references/a24/a24_trailer_0115_blue_painters_tape.jpg`.

Why more media: the on-disk pages are glossy finished interiors. They show finished transitions (portals, bands, soffits) but not the **trim hardware** (beads, reveals, frames, reducers) or **renovation evidence**. The items below close those two gaps. Priority 1 = needed for the Figma "real buildings" slide; 2 = nice to have.

Size advice: for Commons files take the 1920 px thumbnail (`/thumb/…/1920px-…`), not the 25–31 MB original. For archive.org catalogues take single pages, not the whole PDF.
