## 2. Look rules

- **Level surfaces are the game's own:** `L0_Wallpaper`, `L0_Carpet`, `L0_Ceiling` (0.6 grid, one troffer per cell, LMS §4) for Level 0; `Office_Wall`, `Office_Carpet`, `Office_Ceiling`, `Kit_CubiclePanel*`, `Kit_PanelPost*` for the Office; `RunB_BlockLower`, `RunB_BlockUpper`, `RunB_FloorWax`, `RunB_Soffit`, `RunB_GalvSteel` for Run!. The banal kit at the wrong scale is the drama (`20` V4), never a new material. Lamps: each level's own fixture and lamp values (LMS §4), never a bare spot under a lens quad.
- **Wall grime follows world height:** the game's wall shader grimes a floor band 0-0.35 m and a ceiling band 1.6 m under `_CeilingHeight`, in absolute world metres. Every shell renderer gets `_CeilingHeight` = its room's ceiling in world metres through a MaterialPropertyBlock, as the map does (LMS §5); renderers whose floor is not at world y 0 (every floor above or below ±0: SP2's F1-F3 and gallery, SP3's pits, platforms, galleries and patches below, SP4's pit, raised floor and upper floors, SP5's ramp and dock) also get `_FloorGrime` 0, since the shader has no floor offset, and the walls under SP5's sloped soffit `_CeilingGrime` 0 (the shader grimes walls only, and one `_CeilingHeight` cannot follow a slope).
- **Pool tile reads as tile:** porcelain white, base about (0.93, 0.95, 0.96), smoothness 0.85 (Red 10-31 17:19); 150 mm (6 in) tiles, 5 mm cool grey-green grout, per-tile tone ±1.2 %, per-tile normal tilt about 0.5°, a cushion edge in the normal (the `61c` TILE recipe, on a 1.5 m sheet of 10 × 10 tiles so it divides 3 m, LMS §5 `TileAlign`, `40` §11.7; 2048 px is enough).
