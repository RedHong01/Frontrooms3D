## Seam implication

The map tells FrontRoomsKeyHud what the aim is on: none, a locked door (zone shape, colour and number, have-key), or a broken door. It also signals the events: E pressed with no key, E on a broken door, unlock begins (`IsHeldForKey`), the pickup hand-off at HoldUntil, and aim leaves. The HUD owns every crosshair-state visual and its timing. Gameplay stays in the map.
