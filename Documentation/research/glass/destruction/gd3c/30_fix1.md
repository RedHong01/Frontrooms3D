## 8. Note for the G14 track (not implemented here)

- Floor glass and teeth carry `FrontRoomsMetalGlassTarget`, but they appear after the chunk scan, so they enter the ray-traced reflections only if a late rescan sees them.
- To have them reflect and be reflected deterministically:
  - register the window's teeth and floor renderers on `FrontRoomsGlassDebrisWorld.Settled(glass, floor)` (the same event fires on a return);
  - drop them on `FrontRoomsGlassBreakable.Released`.
- A stage-3 window never raises `MeshesReady` any more, so G14's stage queue only sees standing panes.
