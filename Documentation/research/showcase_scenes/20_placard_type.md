## 1. The system

- **Faces (the game's UI set, `Assets/Resources/Fonts`):** Bayon for a kind or room name in capitals; IBM Plex Mono Medium for codes and digits (Bayon has no figures); IBM Plex Mono Regular for Try lines and the foot; Noto Sans SC Regular for Chinese body and Bold for titles and Red's questions. No period faces: placards stand outside the 1990 fiction.
- **Colours:** panel albedo `#161513` (0.086, 0.082, 0.075), matte; text unlit `#F4F1E8` paper (the UI's Paper), so it reads in every light state; labels and the foot unlit `#96938B`; the UI accent `#F4DF3B` for 要你决定, its bar, question numbers and the live state. No other colour, no backing card behind the panel, no shadow on the text.
- **The camera:** the game camera's `fieldOfView` is 76° vertical (`FrontRooms3DGame.cs:515`), so f = 540 / tan 38° = 691.2 px at 1080p; the eye stands 1.62 m. v1 took 76° as the diagonal (f ≈ 1410 px): its pixel figures were 2.04 × too large. VL504 corrects VL500.
- **Size rule:** each kind has a FAR distance, where its identity must read (number, name, title), and a NEAR distance, where the player steps up to read the rest, as at a museum label; distances run from the eye to the panel's centre. At 1080p: Chinese em at least 16 px; Latin running text x-height at least 8 px (Plex Mono about 15.5 px); words in capitals, cap height at least 11 px. These are stricter than the Smithsonian guide's x-height of 1 % of the distance at every distance here.
- **Phones:** the same placards. At the same distance an iPhone draws them smaller (场景 3: C's Chinese is 4.3 pt in v1, about 7.5 pt in v2), so a phone player steps closer; parity is in the content, not the pixel size.
- **Code chip:** the station or district code in Plex Mono Medium inside a 1.4 mm paper keyline, radius 3 mm, padding 6 mm (9 mm on E): the keycap grammar of the prompt chips, so a code reads as "say this".
- **Setting:** flush left, ragged right; no tracking on CJK; Chinese breaks by filling the line (body) or after a phrase mark (a question: after ，), a full-width mark never starts a line, no lone last character; a question wraps hanging after its number; a full-width stop mark (，。、；：？！) at a line's end may hang into the right margin (标点悬挂: the measure is the line without its trailing stop marks; C 2.7 and 4.1 hang their 。 / ； 1-3.5 mm); brackets and quotes（）「」《》 stay inside the measure, as in clreq's hanging rule (4.1's ） fits at 699 of 720). A number, its space and its unit stay on one line ("0.3 米", "24 米"). Plex Mono Try steps joined by ` · `, one action a step; Try rows wrap at a word space, at the Try pitch. **Bayon has no U+00B7:** in a Bayon line ` · ` is drawn as Bayon's own period with its baseline raised .413 × cap (the period's ink, 0 to .1211 em, centred on the cap middle; Bayon cap .6958 em), a Bayon word space each side; one helper for every Bayon line (today C 1.4 / 2.4 / 3.4 / 4.4, `ALCOVE · …`; `images/placard_C_dot.jpg`, VL507). Not the house fallback (Bayon → Plex Mono Regular at Bayon's size: a low round dot in a 48 mm gap at cap 30) and not a Plex dot (at true size it reads a step lighter than Bayon's stems; at NEAR both are 3-4 px).
- **Production:** world-space UI text (as the game's other UI `Text`). **Raster density, per line (v2.2):** the legacy font atlas has no mipmaps (bilinear only), so each line is rastered at twice the 1080p screen's density where that line has to read, ρ = 2 f / (1000 d), f = 691.2 px: at most 2x minified there (no texel skipped), and 1:1 on a 2160p screen. C, D and the floor tag are read at one distance: d = NEAR (C 1.396, D and tag as built). B, A and E are read at two: the lines that identify the sign from afar (B number, name, Chinese; A title, Chinese, plan numbers; E code and title) take d = FAR: B .46080, A .45474, E .55296 px/mm, 2.00x at FAR and 0.80x / 1.03x / 1.13x at NEAR (B's large lines 1.25x magnified up close, soft but whole). The lines read stepping up (B kinds, foot, arrow; A legend and controls; E body and questions) take the geometric mean d = √(FAR × NEAR): B .72859, A .63277, E .73630, 1.26x / 1.44x / 1.50x at NEAR and 3.16x / 2.78x / 2.66x at FAR, where they are under the floor and only texture (they may sparkle as you walk). The number over an entry keeps .35 (F9). One value per kind sent the identifying lines 3.16x (B) and 2.78x (A) at FAR, past the 2.25x limit: jagged from the lane. On 1440p the FAR lines are 1.5x, on 2160p 1.0x; the NEAR lines turn 0.95x / 0.63x (soft on a 4K screen). If Red's walk shows that sparkle or softness, the fix is baked faces with mipmaps (the placards are static text), not another density. Noto Sans SC's subset carries the tables' characters (设置菜单 11-03 23:00, `Tools/localization/extra_chars/zh-Hans.txt`; all 318 non-ASCII characters of the inc 8b table are in main's Regular and Bold, checked 11-04): re-run `Tools/localization/subset_fonts.py` when a table gains characters. No OS fonts: the iPad and iPhone builds must draw the same face.
