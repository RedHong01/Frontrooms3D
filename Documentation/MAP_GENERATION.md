# FrontRooms map generation

The design plan replaces the straight train of rooms with an endless Level 0
maze. The generator produces it as data (cells, edges, zones, keys);
`FrontRoomsMapWorld` builds it as geometry around the player. The main game
(`FrontRooms3D.unity`) plays in it: the title is still the looping room
stream, and pressing Space hands the player control in the stream room they
are watching. The maze lies behind that corridor's next shut door.

## Main game flow

1. **Title.** `FrontRoomsRoomStream` loops the Lobby corridor with the logo, unchanged.
2. **Space.** No glide and no white-out: the player takes over where the camera is.
   - **Stream:** it ends at its first door that is still shut (`FrontRoomsRoomStream.EndStreamAt`). Rooms past that door are put away, and nothing recycles or rebases any more.
   - **Placement:** `FrontRoomsMapWorld.CreateEmbedded` attaches the maze behind that door.
     - Its root is the nearest multiple of 192 m where the door opens onto a cell (the title runs at x 256.5 = a cell centre, z 0 = room ends on cell lines).
     - The five cells across the door are Standard-height Level 0, matching the stream room's 2.9 m ceiling and paper.
     - The cell ahead is open and leads on for 150+ cells.
     - G1 (tutorial phase 0): the door cell's west edge is Open, so the evacuation plan hangs on the start wall west of the door (P1 west, `FrontRoomsPlacard`) and its YOU ARE HERE is true on every seed. A Standard row without it is only a fallback (logged). `FrontRoomsExitPlanner.RootFor` is the same search in cells (`StartDoorWestOpen`); the map verification fails a seed whose floor 1 has no such root. Floors 2+ (`SeedFor`) prefer the same rule beside the stairwell's north door (`StairDoorPlacardWest`: the door cell's west edge Open and a wall on the south edge of the cell west of it, where the B1+ sheet hangs): the first of the 256 floor seeds that fits it, else the first Standard one (`FloorSeedPlan.placardWest` false; about 1 floor start in 200 on the 100-seed check, counted as `placardWestMissing`; the placard mount skips the sheet there). `FrontRooms3DGame.StartStairwell` names that door.
   - **Start area:** the stream rooms are the map's start area (`SetStartArea`). The map builds nothing there and walls it off on three sides. The stream room's end wall, carried out by two facade strips, forms the fourth side with the door in it. `IsBuilt` and `PassageBetween` treat it as no map.
   - **Streaming:** the maze streams in at the normal per-frame budget round the door (`StreamFocus`, `Begin(player, false)`). Map lamps whose light would reach through the stream walls are held dark (`StartLampsNorth/South`).
   - **The door:** it opens once everything in sight through it is built and furnished (`ReadyAround(BuildRadius)`, at most 3 s after Space).
3. **Leaving the start rooms.**
   - Once the player is 4 m into the maze, the door swings shut and stays shut.
   - The stream's lamps fade out, and the maze's lamps beside the rooms fade in.
   - The rooms behind it are destroyed one per frame. The last room goes, and the start area is handed back to the map, once every chunk round it has been dropped.
4. **Play.**
   - WASD and mouse to move and look. Shift sprints on 5 s of stamina, which refills after 1 s. Run dry, the player is winded: no sprint until one segment (1 s) is back, so holding Shift on an empty bar makes no sprint steps or noise. The sound layer reads the state, read-only: `FrontRooms3DGame.PlayerStamina01`, `PlayerWinded`, `PlayerSprinting`.
   - E opens or shuts a door; holding E for 1 s breaks glass, and walking into the broken frame climbs through it.
   - **Doors are double-acting** (A12, Red 2026-10-22: "全部按推荐"; replaces the single-acting rule of 10-02). A leaf swings both ways (no stops), 95° at rest, and always away from whoever moves it. From the frame it goes to the far side of its opener (`SwingAwayFrom`, also called for a locked rattle, a key turn and a Relay break). A leaf out of the frame goes on away from them: further open when the opener stands behind it, or back through the frame to the far side when they stand ahead of it (`DoorMotion.Through`: at the open's starting speed, never latching on the way). The side it went to is kept by edge (`doorSides`), so open and broken doors are rebuilt on it; a door never opened rests on its edge's hash side (`FixedSwing`). It stays where it is left (no closer). E presses the lever: the leaf leaves the frame `Open.SwingStart` (0.1 s) later, eases out over 0.55 s with a 2° overshoot and rests at 0.75 s; `DoorMoved` fires as it leaves the frame, and a second E meanwhile does nothing. Opens never pull (no pull beat, no `DoorPulled`). Shutting is today's 0.55 s smoothstep, landing in the frame, never through it, with `DoorLatched`. Shutting from the side the leaf is open to, with the body in its sweep, raises `DoorPulled(door, clear spot)` (latch side, out of the leaf's 1.0 m sweep, inside the keep-clear strip), and the game steps the player there at `PullStepSpeed` (4 m/s) for at most `PullStepSeconds` (0.35 s); Red's A12 answer keeps this step. A spot further than that, or a chasing or seeing Relay, means no step, and the leaf waits on the body. A moving leaf stops on the player's or the Relay's body and goes on once it is clear. `PassageBetween` reads a built door, broken or not, as Open from 70° (a 0.6 m body fits), ClosedDoor below. A locked door rattles: two jolts of the leaf child away from the trier. `FaceOf` is geometric (S = the door root's +X face, the kit's authored face; the double-acting rig is not mirrored).
   - **Keys (C14, Red 2026-10-21):** every key is taken by looking at it and pressing E ("E · TAKE KEY"; `KeyPickupNeedsE`, set true by the game before the first chunk builds). Phones too (交互设计, 2026-10-22): USE says TAKE on a key under the crosshair, and a tap on a key takes it (`KeyOnRay` + `AimKind.TakeKey`). With `doorsNeedKeys` off in the level profile (the default) they are shown but not required. Each map owns a private copy of the key material (codex audit C1, 2026-10-21), so a restart no longer turns the keys magenta.
     - Door-shot step 5 (2026-10-08): at contact the key is held (`keysHeld`, so a rebuild cannot respawn it) and `KeyPickupStarted(zone, pos)` fires. The game's `KeyPresenter` (`FrontRoomsDoorShot.BeginKeyPickup`) then decides.
     - With no key model and `ShowPlaceholderKey` off, which is shipped play today, it declines. The cube is killed and `KeyTaken` fires in the same frame, as before, and the key panel shows at once.
     - With a model (a dressed key's `IFrontRoomsKeyRig`, or the dev placeholder: *FrontRooms/Map/Show placeholder key*):
       - the key travels to the held point and `KeyTaken` fires at its 0.25 s arrival;
       - it pockets at 0.85 s (`Beat(KeyPickup, Pocket)`) and is gone at 0.95 s;
       - the key panel fades in after it, and a 0.2 s aim assist (±6°, only with the key within 30° of the view and the mouse idle) turns the base pitch toward it.
     - `KeyPickupNeedsE` (off) makes keys look+E pickups: a trigger the gameplay rays ignore, "E · TAKE KEY".
   - With `doorsNeedKeys` on, a shut door needs the key of the zone you stand in (`KeyFor(door, from)`, the one place the key rule lives; `LockedByRule(door)` is `InteriorDoorsLocked` for inner doors, `ExitLocked` for the EXIT). On a floor with a station the floor key replaces the zone keys (M3: next section but one, "Floor 1's start group and the floor key").
     - **No key:** `DoorLocked`, then `DoorRattled(door, lock point)`. A second try within `Rattle.Duration` (0.50 s since 2026-10-21, the HUD's deny window: one press, one rattle, one HUD tug; crosshair grammar H2) does nothing.
     - **With the key, in the game:** E holds the door for the unlock shot ("the head dip", `FrontRoomsDoorShot`, door-shot build A, 2026-10-08). `Use` returns `KeyTurn`, and the game's `UseDoor` starts the shot (`BeginUnlock`).
       - At the 0.86 s commit the map raises `DoorUnlocked(door, zone, keyhole)`, and the open follows with the latch drawn at 1.00 s. Every unlock is a push (A12): the leaf goes away from the player from either face.
       - A cancel before the commit leaves the door locked. A cancel after it leaves it unlocked and **shut**, except a second E, which opens it.
       - The hold has a watchdog: the owner must call `RefreshKeyTurn` every tick, or the hold is resolved after `Unlock.Duration` + 0.5 s, with one warning.
     - **Without a key-turn owner** (the walker, the Level Designer preview, `TryOpenDoor`, tools): the door unlocks and opens in the same call.
     - `UnlockSwingDelay` is gone. From then on the door is unlocked from both sides.
     - Every open raises `DoorOpenBegan(door, Use | Tools | Unlock, latchDrawn)`.
     - Keys stay off in shipped play (`Levels/FrontRoomsLevel0.asset`). To try them: *FrontRooms/Map/Play with keys on* (a SessionState flag the autopilot ignores), or `DoorsNeedKeysForTools` in tests. With keys off, the autopilot fails a run if any Unlock shot starts.
   - **Broken doors** (Red, 2026-10-14; RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN.md §10). A door the Relay broke stays broken, open and crooked, for the run: kept by edge through drops, rebuilds and revisit shifts, cleared only by `ResetDoorForTools` (tests). It is an Open passage at 0 m in the path field, so a broken locked door lets anyone through. E on it is a failed try, never a door verb: `Use` returns `Broken` and raises `DoorBrokenTried(door, player position)`, with no door event and no Relay noise; a try within `BrokenDoor.Duration` (0.55 s, the HUD's won't-shut window) of the last returns None. `Describe` gives it no prompt (the BROKEN / WON'T SHUT strings are 平面视觉's); the HUD keys on the kind below. On desktop the game aims it anyway (FIX_CARD_VL217 §B, until KeyHud B): the prompt reads BROKEN, then WON'T SHUT for the 0.55 s after a try, and E on it is the try. Phones keep `Describe`'s null: USE finds the door on the same ray (`UpdateMobileTouchPrompt`).
   - **Crosshair kinds** (H1, research/computer_ui/30_crosshair_grammar.md §7, 2026-10-21). `Kind(collider, from, out keyZone, out haveKey)` returns an `AimKind` (append only): Open and Shut (a door E opens or shuts), Locked and LockedHaveKey (a shut door that needs a key, without or with it), Broken, Glass and GlassTap (Tap to break), TakeKey (a look+E key); Read and Examine belong to EXAMINE's aim registry; Pry and PryAlarmed (a sash latch, C12/C13) and AlarmDoor (an armed alarm push-bar door that needs no key, C16) came later. It is not None exactly when `Describe` has a prompt, plus Broken. For a locked door, keyZone is the zone whose key opens it from `from` (KeyFor's rule) and haveKey whether that key is held. The HUD and the touch layer key their states on it, never on the prompt string.
   - **Outlets** (the visual chat's planner, contract C-1, 2026-10-22): `BuildEdge` lists both faces of every built edge (`AddWallFaces`: start, direction, normal, kind, opening span, edge id, side, cell, foreign, room, zone), and `BuildInto` calls `FrontRoomsWallFixtures.Install` once per chunk, after the zone grades and before Furnish. Nothing on door or window faces, none round the start area; hashed by seed + edge + side, so rebuilds and shifts keep them. No colliders, shadows or GameObjects. Checked with research/outlets/data/30_FrontRoomsOutletContractProbe (10 seeds).
   - Esc (or losing focus) pauses; `FrontRooms3DGame.Paused(bool)` reports it, for the sound layer. Doors freeze with the game (`map.DoorsPaused` whenever the phase is not Playing): a swing, a leaf jolt and the key-turn watchdog stop under the pause card instead of finishing. On desktop the pause card shows the controls legend (T5) over three lines, ESC  RESUME, R  RESTART and O  SETTINGS; the mouse works there and in settings (hover marks a line or chooses a row, a click is its key), and R asks first: R AGAIN TO RESTART for 2 s (FIX_CARD_VL217 A2, A3).
   - The HUD shows the zone name and meta line, the prompt and hold bar, and the stamina segments under the crosshair. The hint line shows only flashes (M1, 2026-10-22: the first-run CalmHint card is gone; the controls are on the title and the pause card). On desktop it has no dark card: paper with a 1 px keyline (A6). The prompt under the crosshair has the same keyline on desktop and phones (hud_build DECISIONS #4, 2026-10-29). The meta line reads ZONE nn / STANDARD 3.0 M: no TIER (C04, Red 2026-10-21). The Relay's state and distance are an assist, off by default (Red, 2026-10-03): Esc → O (settings) → RELAY READOUT, or T, turns them on, remembered across runs. Captions (an assist, off by default) show up to three lines above the hint card, each with its direction (AHEAD, LEFT, RIGHT, BEHIND); the sound layer posts them with `FrontRoomsCaptions.Post(text, source, seconds)`. Settings: `DISPLAY_SETTINGS.md`.
   - **Camera.** The player camera is a rig (`FrontRoomsCameraRig`). Gameplay (the aim ray and the Relay's view of the player) reads only `BaseEye`; the aim (`AimCast`) takes the nearest solid hit that is not the player's own body (A10, 2026-10-22: from about 33° down the ray used to stop on the player's CharacterController): the body plus 1.62 m, base yaw and pitch, and the window-climb duck. Shots, shakes, offsets and FOV changes are picture only. They are clamped 0.1 m short of walls with a spherecast and scaled by the Camera motion setting. During a shot, E and S are swallowed: a cancel after the delay; one E in the last 0.2 s of a door shot (Open, Unlock) is kept as a buffered push instead (`BufferedPush`, read and cleared by the shot's owner). A chasing or seeing Relay frees the player from a shot at once. The exception is the unlock (`FrontRoomsDoorShot.Threatened`, fed by Game's `UnlockThreat`): before its commit only a chase, or sight within 6 m, cancels it; after the commit any chase or sight frees the player and the door stays shut. Beat times live in `FrontRoomsShotTimings`, the values from the visual chat's audit §3, except Glass, which is being redesigned. EXAMINE's rig half is in (step 1 of research/inspect/contracts/map_inspect.md, 2026-10-21): `ShotKind.Inspect` (appended), `ShotSpec.fullPose` (pose and FOV not scaled by Camera motion; a cut at Off), `ShotEase.Sine` with its own rotation return (`rotBlendOut`, which `EndShot` never leaves slower than the end asked for), `fovMin` for Inspect only, a 16-hit camera buffer, `ShotHandle.Weight/EasedWeight/RotationWeight`, and `FrontRoomsShotTimings.Inspect`. Every other shot is unchanged. Step 3 (the game phase) landed 2026-10-22: `Phase.Inspect` with `Time.timeScale` 0, E on an EXAMINE target (`FrontRoomsInspect.Find`, the visual chat's aim boxes) opens the close-up through `CanShowInspect` (no prompt while the Relay chases, breaks a door or is in view) and `CanPressInspect` (latch, 0.30 s cooldown, no shot running); E, S or Esc leave after 0.20 s; R is ignored in it; focus loss pauses and resumes into it. Control is back the frame the close-up starts home, and its return leg no longer holds `HudFade`, so the crosshair and HUD are back 0.15 s later while the camera finishes its 0.40 s return (O-25, crosshair grammar §2; it was +0.533 s). The design and the decisions are in research/inspect/ (map_inspect.md).
   - **Shakes** (each falls linearly to nothing over its decay time): a 0.3° push jolt on the lever (`DoorHandleTurned`) and on the latch (`DoorLatched`) of a door within 3.6 m; the locked rattle's two 0.25° forward-pitch kicks (`rig.Kick`, since door-shot build A) at `Rattle.Jolt1` and `Jolt2`; the Relay's blows and break within 8 m (0.2° at the first blow to 0.6° at the last, 1.0° at the break).
   - **Glass** (Red, 2026-10-03, the visual chat's glass destruction plan): panes break from 1.2 m (everything else keeps 2.4 m). A hold starts only on a fresh E press on the pane (or a tap, in tap mode, where the prompt reads TAP E; one tap = one strike). E-down steps the body in to stand 0.55 m from the pane (at most 0.65 m forward, 0.25 m sideways) and starts the shot "Brace, strike, flinch" (`FrontRoomsGlassShot`, timings in `FrontRoomsShotTimings.GlassBreak`): three strikes land on the 0.35 / 0.70 / 1.00 beats, then a follow-through, a flinch and a look at the sill, back by 1.65 s. Move is held through the hold and look stays within ±5°; both are held 1.00–1.10 s; until 1.30 asking to move (a key pressed or still held), and until 1.40 a look over 3°, ends the shot. At Camera motion Off look stays free and nothing is held after the shatter; the hold itself still holds the body. A re-press while the shot blends back picks it up again without a snap. In tap mode, asking to move between taps lets go of the pane. A door pull's step clearing a leaf is never overwritten by the step-in. Letting go cancels the shot and the hold drops back to the stage reached (0 / 0.35 / 0.70: cracks do not heal). The Relay's sight or a chase demotes the shot to half-strength lean and jabs instead of cancelling the hold; after the shatter it skips the shot. `FrontRoomsGlassShot.Beat(beat, intensity)` fires on each beat for the post stack. In tap mode, when a tap's credit runs out the stress stops (`PauseHold`: `GlassHoldReleased` once) and the progress stays for the next tap; looking away resets it to the stage reached.
   - **Glass contract (map side):** every window has an unscaled root `Window {a}-{b}` (the opening's centre at floor level, +Z into cell b), broken ones too; the gameplay box `Window pane {a}-{b}` hangs under it, and only it goes at the shatter. When the visual chat's `FrontRoomsGlassBreakable` exists it is added to the root and the box's renderer is turned off. Events: `GlassHoldStarted(window, impact, seed)` at E-down (the base-eye hit kept 0.2 m inside the exposed glass, fixed once cracked), `WindowHeld(window, progress)`, `GlassCracked(window, 1/2)` once each, `WindowShattered(window, impact, impulse)` with `GlassBroken`, `WindowBuilt(window, record)` and `WindowReleased(window)`. The break record (`GlassBreakRecord`: stage, seed, impact and its UV over the exposed glass, rotation, side) is kept by edge, so a rebuilt pane comes back cracked.
   - **Sash pry (C12, Relay v2 step V6; RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN §5, §10), behind the profile flag `sashPry`, off in `FrontRoomsLevel0.asset` until the latch's look lands.** Off, nothing here exists. On: every plain window's pane gets a sibling under the root, `Window latch {a}-{b}` (`Window.latch`), a solid box with no renderer, 0.36 × 0.14 × 0.10 m on the meeting rail (y 1.175), proud of the pane on both faces, so E aims at it from either room. `Describe` and `Kind` answer `HOLD E  ·  PRY SASH` (PROPOSED) and `AimKind.Pry` (`PryAlarmed` is appended for V7a; the tap prompt is the game's, as for glass). It keeps the pane's 1.2 m reach. The game routes E to `Pry` (`UpdatePry`) or to the pane's hold: no shot, nothing locks the body, and walking off lets go. `SashPryHoldSeconds` is 4 s; progress banks by edge through releases, drops, rebuilds and shifts (`sashHolds`, GD3c's pattern); a tap banks a third of the hold. `SashPryProgress(window, 0-1)` every held frame, `SashPryReleased(window)`, and `SashPried(window)` as it gives: pane and latch go, the root stays, the edge joins `priedWindows`, for good: an Open passage at cost 0. `IsOpenWindow` (broken or pried) is the climb's test; `IsBrokenWindow` stays broken only, and `IsSashPried(edge)` tells a rebuilt window's look that its sash is up. A shatter takes the latch and its progress; a cracked pane can still be pried. Pry and pane never touch each other's progress, stages or events, so 4 s on the latch never raises `GlassBroken`. The game's `OnSashPried` makes the noise: v1 `Noise(8)`, v2 loudness 8 and Attention +8, from `tuning.pryLoudness`. The hunter, flag on, crosses a broken or pried window by a 0.6 s frame step (`WindowStepSeconds`, `WindowStepping`, `WindowStepProgress`, `WindowStepped`, `WindowSteps`), from where a climb would start (0.95 m short of the line, 0.45 m off its middle) to 0.75 m past it. The step runs to its end, the state's own clocks waiting, and only a placement or going off the map ends it early. A noise mid-step re-plans; the body lands, and the plan goes on from the far cell, so a way back over the sill is a step too.
   - **Foiled windows, the one trigger budget and auto Relay entries (C13, Relay v2 step V7a; RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN §6, §10, §12), behind the profile flag `foil`, off in `FrontRoomsLevel0.asset` until the foil's look lands.** Off, none of it exists: no planner, no budget unless Run! rooms make one, no auto entries, the same maps and validator results. On: (1) **The budget.** `FrontRoomsTriggerBudget` (the cache's `Budget`) is the map's ONE trigger budget, shared by Run! rooms and foiled windows: each zone rolls once (pure in the seed and the zone, the salt Run! rooms always rolled with), and a roll under 1 / zonesPerDevice at the zone's stored tier (3 / 2.5 / 2 / 1.75 / 1.5, the tier table's `zonesPerDevice`) is the zone's one slot. A slot holds one device of one kind (the ledger: `Take`, `HolderOf`, `Count`): a Run! room in a Standard zone, a foiled window in a Tall zone. The Run! plan is unchanged by it (the same roll, chance and arithmetic). (2) **Foil** (`FrontRoomsFoil`, the cache's `Foil`). A window always has one Tall side, so it is its Tall zone's. Per Tall zone, decided once at the first question after `Begin` (Ready) and kept for the floor: the candidates are its chunk-border windows (`BorderEdge`, pure in the seed, so no rebuild or revisit shift moves them; windows inside a chunk change with its revision and are always plain; windows on the start area or X are not in play), ranked by edge hash. The lowest-ranked window of each border (Tall zone, neighbour zone) stays plain, then the lowest-ranked others until min(2, count) are plain. With the zone's slot (or forced by tools), the highest-ranked window that is neither plain nor excluded is foiled: one per zone, and the ledger holds the slot for Foil. Excluded (RELAY 6): the first zone (the start door's, either side), 15 cells (Manhattan) of the start door, and `DeviceExcluded`: the start area and X with a ring of one cell, N, and with a station (floor 1's, or a floor below's: BS1-BS3) its footprint and ring, its protected cells, its strip and its walk (D..N, or the arrival door to the station). `Window.foiled` says it on every build. (3) **The device.** A foiled window's latch (with `sashPry`) answers `AimKind.PryAlarmed` and `HOLD E  ·  PRY SASH  ·  ALARMED` (tap `TAP E  ·  PRY SASH  ·  ALARMED`; PROPOSED). Its pane giving (at the shatter) or its sash giving trips the device once per window and floor: `DeviceTripped(point, id)` (the opening's centre, `foil:(x, y)-(x, y)`), raised just before `GlassBroken` or `SashPried`. The game makes ONE device call for it: v1 (the bridge, if foil is switched on before the flip) a `Noise` of `deviceLoudness` (45) at the device; under `FRONTROOMS_RELAY_V2` the director's `DeviceTrigger(point, id)` and the on-map ears at 45 (a foiled pane's `OnGlassBroken` skips its own glass call). The route never crosses a foiled window: glass, whole or open, is not a crossing for it. (4) **Auto Relay entries** (`FrontRoomsAutoEntries`, read by `RelayEntries`): per built zone, every dead-end tip (one walkable edge), and while no two entries are 10 cells apart (Manhattan) the corridor ends (a straight corridor cell whose neighbour along it opens three or more ways), then any other cell, whichever make the farthest pair; never on reserved or `DeviceExcluded` cells; tag `vent` (Low, Standard) or `doorway` (Tall), which `Arrived` carries; Run!'s 9-cell rule applies to them as to markers.    - **Alarm push-bar doors (C16, Red's Q6 pick, the tier-1 trigger kind beside windows; Relay v2 step V7b; RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN §6), behind the profile flag `alarmDoor`, off in `FrontRoomsLevel0.asset` until the door's look lands.** Off, none of it exists: no planner, no budget unless Run! rooms or foil make one, `Door.alarm` false everywhere, the same maps, prompts and game. On: (1) **The planner** (`FrontRoomsAlarmDoors`, the cache's `AlarmDoors`), the third kind in V7a's ONE budget and ledger (`TriggerDeviceKind.AlarmDoor`). A door joins a Low and a Standard zone. ## Level profile

Every tunable number of the level is one asset, `Assets/Levels/FrontRoomsLevel0.asset` (`FrontRoomsLevelProfile`; **FrontRooms → Map → Select level profile**). The `FrontRooms 3D` object's `Level Profile` field points at it; the test scene, the debug window and the 100-seed check resolve the same asset.

- `generation`: zone shares, maze and room grammar, exits, pillars, Office share (`MapSettings`). Its seed is the preview seed for the tools.
- Run: `runSeed` (0 = new maze each run), `buildRadius` (1–3), `chunksPerFrame`, `shiftAfterSeconds`, `doorsNeedKeys`.
- Light budget: `lightRadius`, `shadowRadius`.
- Dressing: `dressOffices`, `pileChance`, `transitions`, `renovationShare` (level transitions, below).
- Relay v2 content, off until each look lands: `sashPry` (C12, the quiet sash pry), `foil` (C13, foiled windows in the one trigger budget with Run! rooms, the plain-window rule, auto Relay entries; V7a), `alarmDoor` (C16, alarm push-bar doors in the same budget; V7b). `runRooms` (L7) has its own switches. The Run! rethink adds `runDoorEntrance`, `runRecede` and `runRamps`, all off (section "Run! rooms (L7) and the Run! rethink").

Edits made in Play mode are kept (it is an asset) and apply from the next run; a running map works on a copy. With no asset assigned the code defaults apply. The fixed geometry (cell, wall, openings, troffer, bodies) is `ModuleUnits`, documented in `LEVEL_MODULE_SPEC.md`.

## Grid

- A **cell** is a 3 m square, one corridor wide. Cell `(x, y)` covers world X `[3x, 3x+3)` and Z `[3y, 3y+3)`.
- A **chunk** is 8 × 8 cells (24 m) and is the unit that is built and dropped around the player.
- Every edge between two neighbouring cells has one **kind**: `Open`, `Arch` (a doorless doorway: blocks sight, not movement), `Wall`, `Door`, `Window`.

## Level 0 grammar

The Backrooms are walls and doorways, not columns. Each chunk is carved in two passes:

1. **Maze.** A random depth-first spanning tree over the 64 cells. Its edges are never walls, so every cell stays reachable, and its long branches read as corridors. Some tree edges become doorways (standard 30 %, low 25 %, tall 10 %), the rest stay open corridor.
2. **Rooms.** Rectangles carved on top of the maze, with every edge inside them open: standard zones get two rooms of 2–4 cells, low zones two of 2–3, tall zones one hall of 5–7.

Every other edge inside a zone is a wall, a doorway or open: standard 82 / 10 / 8 % (the maze), low 50 / 20 / 30 % (it leaks), tall 30 / 10 / 60 % (halls). Doorways get a random width (1.1–1.8 m) and an off-centre position from the edge hash, so no two line up.

Columns stand on the world 6 m grid (cell corners with both indices even), only inside rooms that are one open space, at least 3 × 3 cells, never in Low zones. A qualifying room rolls once (Level 0 25 %, Office 75 %, Tall hall 70 %) and then fills every grid corner inside it: 0.6 m columns in Level 0, 0.9 m in Offices (with bulkheads between them) and tall halls. See `LEVEL_MODULE_SPEC.md` §3.

## Room modules (Level Designer)

Rooms authored in the Level Designer (`LEVEL_DESIGNER.md`) can replace carved rooms. The level profile's `modules` list is the library; `generation.moduleChance` (0.3) and `moduleTier` set how often and which.

1. After the maze, the rooms and the columns, each generated room that is one open space (no later room cuts into it, every cell the same height and theme) rolls `moduleChance`.
2. If it hits, the candidates are the modules with the room's height and theme whose tier range includes `moduleTier`, in every quarter turn they allow (`allowRotate`) that fits the room. One is chosen by weight (a module's weight is shared between its fitting turns).
3. It is stamped (`RoomModuleStamp`) at a hashed spot inside the room: inner and perimeter edges inside the chunk as authored, the map's rule at zone borders and on the chunk border, columns per the module, walls reopened if the chunk was cut apart.
4. **Markers.** A module's **key spot** takes its zone's key when the key's cell (the cell holding the zone's site) lies in the module's room and the spot's cell is in the same zone: the key lies there, still, turned as the marker says (on a revisit shift it may move within its room). Its **Relay entries** are where the Relay prefers to appear (see the Relay above). The Office kit and piles keep a metre round the key and 0.8 m round each entry clear.
5. When it is built, a module prop is left out (with a console warning) if it would block a real opening, stands in a column, or stands on a prop left out (a desk-top item goes with its desk). A fill (Office kit, pile) sees the module's inner walls as obstacles and keeps its inner doorways clear.

All of it is a function of the seed, the chunk and its revision, with the library in a stable order (by asset name), so neighbours agree, rebuilds are identical and a revisit shift may bring a different module. The 100-seed check runs with the profile's library and reports how many modules it placed; outside Unity it was also run with 14 random modules at chance 0.7 (3,806 placed, 100/100). The debug map outlines placed modules in orange.

## Zones and heights

Each chunk owns one random site. A cell belongs to the zone of the nearest site,
so zones are irregular and cross chunk borders. Each zone rolls a ceiling class:
`Low` 2.4 m, `Standard` 2.9 m, `Tall` 5.4 m (shares 35 / 55 / 10 %).

- Where the height changes, an edge is a `Wall` unless it opens as an exit, and the taller side decides the exit: **Door** between low and standard, **Window** whenever one side is tall, except for one arch per tall zone (next).

## Guarantees

- **Reachable:** the maze tree connects every cell of a chunk, and each chunk border has one required opening, so every cell connects to every other (doors and windows count as passable).
- **Tall zones open on foot (C33):** every Tall zone has its arch to a lower zone, the same on every build, rebuild and revisit shift.
- **Borders agree:** zones, heights, border edges and border corners are pure functions of the seed and world coordinates, so neighbouring chunks agree whichever is built first.
- **A chunk that fails to build** (an exception in `Build`) is undone, logged once and left out until it leaves the build radius, then tried once more; `ReadyAround` and `Settled` count it as done.
- **Revisits shift (decision 2):** a chunk the player has been away from for at least 30 s is rebuilt with a new revision when they come back. The maze, rooms and interior walls change. Borders, zones and keys stay the same. It is always at least 24 m away and inside the fog when it is rebuilt, so the change is never seen.

The visual line builds the member (a painted hollow-metal DA leaf, kick plate, louvre; free and locked rows); the map owns the rule for which map doors wear it.

- **The rule: one Low zone in three is a service zone, and every Level 0 door on its border is back-of-house.** A zone is drawn by `Unit(MapHash(seed, zone id, "BOH1")) < BackOfHouseShare` (1/3), so a service zone is a whole low area about 24 m across: all its doors into Standard Level 0 zones are the steel door, from both sides; the other Low zones keep the walnut door. Low zones percolate into areas of up to ~100 zones on a floor, so the rule draws zones, not areas; zones inside one low area look alike (same height and paper), so a service zone shows only through its doors. Nothing else changes in a service zone: its lamps (Red 10-31, low areas as recommended), paper, carpet and ceiling stay as today. The share is per zone, not tuned toward the EXIT or the station: the door is never a way-finding cue.
- **`FrontRoomsMapWorld.BackOfHouseDoor(Door)`** (`FrontRoomsMapWorld.BackOfHouse.cs`): true for a door between a service zone and a Standard Level 0 zone. False for the EXIT door, a Run! door (`IsRunDoor`), a door with an Office or pool-theme side, a door touching a cell `TransitionExcluded` names (the start area and its stairwell, X and the EXIT's 2-cell approach, the start stairwell door's hold on later floors, the floor-1 station's group and strip, pool cells), a pair of sides that is not Low | Standard, and an edge that is not a door. `BackOfHouseZone(zoneId)` is the zone half (tools, plans).
- **Pure for the floor:** the seed and the two cells' zones decide (read through the generator, so no zone tier is recorded and no Run! zone is met), with the floor's start, EXIT and station, which are set before the first build. No revision, tier or build order: both chunk copies of a border door, every rebuild and every revisit shift agree; the edge test only says whether the edge is a door now. The same on desktop, iPhone and iPad.
- **Precedence** (agreed with the visual line, 11-05): Run / Exit > Office / Pool > back-of-house > Lobby. Their kit asks it in `DoorMemberFor` right after the map hook (`if (map.BackOfHouseDoor(door)) return FrontRoomsDoorMember.BackOfHouse;`), before its Office and Pool tests, which is why the predicate is false wherever those would answer. **Inert** until that line lands: nothing in the game calls it, and every door looks as today. WebGL leaves map doors undressed, as before.
- **Measured** (`FrontRoomsBackOfHouseDoorTests`, 20 seeds × 81 chunks, cache only): 6,651 map doors (4.11 a chunk), 2,220 Office, 4,431 Lobby; 1,534 back-of-house = 34.6 % of today's Lobby doors (23.1 % of all map doors), 76.7 a floor (46–156; 19–64 % of a floor's Lobby doors), 0.95 a chunk; 217 of 656 Low zones with a Lobby door are service zones (33.1 %). ## Lamp overrides (interface v1)

Agreed on 2026-10-03 by the map chat, the wallpaper-print chat and 系统设计 (RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN.md §7.4b; research/wallpaper_motion/20_level_design_phosphor.md §11.1 Step 1). Everything works on each lamp's logical level, which the light, the lens, the hum and the tools all follow. With no override active, both tick paths (desktop and WebGL) are bit-identical to before.

- **Reading:** `LampLevel(cell)` is this frame's level, overrides included; `LampBaseLevel(cell)` is the level before them. Both return `NoLamp` (−1) where no lamp is built.
- **`LampModeOf(cell)`** is pure. It never generates a chunk: for one not generated yet it predicts at the current `GenerationTier` with Auto lamps. With the V5 border rule on (`-lightleadBorders`, `-lightleadSoft`), the border edges come from `EdgeNoGenerate` (codex audit C2, 2026-10-21): a chunk-border edge from the seed (`BorderEdge`), an inner edge from its own generated chunk, Wall for a chunk not generated yet; a build reads exactly what `Cache.Edge` would. A module's Off and the start area read Off.
- **`SetLampMode(cell, mode)`** sets a lamp's temperament for good: Off (the kill: a dark lens, the troffer still there), or a promotion such as Failing; Auto goes back to the map's own. It is kept by cell, like broken doors, so it survives a drop, a rebuild and a revisit shift. A built lamp changes at once and starts a fresh cycle of its new mode. It does nothing in the start area or where a module took the lamp out.
- **`SetLampOverride(cell, LampFx, multiplier, hold, envelope?)`** returns a handle: the lamp eases to that fraction of its own level, holds, and comes back. `hold` may be `PositiveInfinity` (until `RemoveLampOverride(handle)`, which releases it from where it is). Kinds and default envelopes (attack / release): Dip 0.3 / 1.2 s, Sag 0.6 / 1.5 s, Warn 0.08 / 0.2 s. Overlapping overrides take the **lowest** multiplier, never the product. With Reduce flashing on, no attack or release is shorter than 0.5 s. No delay argument: callers schedule their calls and keep any one lamp under 3 changes a second. An envelope may carry a **tail** (M3, `LampEnvelope(attack, release, tail)`): the release is then exp(−u / tail) scaled to reach 1 exactly on time, fast at first and easing out like a ballast recovering; with no tail (every other caller) it is linear and unchanged, and under Reduce flashing it is always linear.
- **`SetLampHold(cell, look, seconds)`** (M1, lamp check `research/lamp_warn/10_lamp_look_check.md`) holds a Stutter or Failing lamp's look at Steady or Dim, crossfaded in the lamp tick over `seconds` (Reduce flashing: at least 0.5 s) from the level it shows: no step. Its mode (`LampModeOf`) never changes and keeps running unseen, so it is where it would have been when let go; fully held it is exactly the Steady or Dim formula, through the flash limiter like the mode itself. Kept by cell like the lamp modes (a rebuilt or shifted chunk keeps it; a lamp not built yet is held at once); every other mode ignores it, so Steady, Dim, Dead and Off lamps are unchanged. `ReleaseLampHold(cell, delay, seconds)` lets go after `delay` (still held) over `seconds` (at least 1 s) to the mode's calm level (Stutter between episodes, Failing's wave with no dropout); the hold is dropped `LampHoldQuiet` (0.7 s) later at the first frame the mode is between events, so the first stutter or dropout it plays again never shares a second with the crossfade. `SetLampHold` again cancels a pending let-go and goes on from where the crossfade is. `LampHeld`, `LampHoldWeight`, `LampHoldCount`, `LampHoldForTools`. NextFloor drops every hold (and every M2 ambient hold).
- **Events:** `LampDipped(cell, light position)` when an override starts on a built lamp, so the fixture can buzz or tick. `FixtureChanged(cell, level)` when a lamp's level crosses 0.15, 0.55 or 0.8 by at least 0.02, never per frame.
- **The lens (V1, lamp check `research/lamp_warn/10_lamp_look_check.md`):** each lamp's lens carries `_FR_LampDip` in its property block, both tick paths: the override layer's multiplier this frame (level / base level while the lamp's own level is lit), exactly 1 with no override, Dead and Off lamps included. The visual chat's lens shader dips the emission with it (`pow(_FR_LampDip, k)`); a lens with no block (the title corridor's) must read 1, so the shader's default is 1. `CheckLampStatesForTools` checks it on every lit lens.
- **Pursuit Step 1:** `FrontRoomsMapPathField` now measures the player-rooted 45 m octile field without generating unbuilt chunks. `FrontRoomsMapHunter` exposes `PathDistanceToPlayer`, `PathOpeningCost`, `WarningDistance`, `WarnStage`, `WarnStageChanged` and the 10 Hz `PlayerSeesRelay` sight hook, plus Relay-rooted measurements for estimate-side decisions; noise falls back to a bounded one-off source field when it is not rooted at the player. The warning stage is data-only and does not drive lamps, audio or other presentation. `WarnStage` follows RELAY_PURSUIT_REDESIGN §9.1 (fixed 2026-10-08):
  - A stage starts on the first sample inside its band (W ≤ 30 m / 18 m); 0 → 2 and 1 → 2 happen at once.
  - The hold is a minimum time from entry (stage 1 ≥ 4 s, stage 2 ≥ 3 s), never an entry delay.
  - After that, a stage ends once W has stayed past its exit band (36 m / 24 m) for 1 s. Stage 2 goes to 0 only if W was also past 36 m for that second; otherwise it drops to 1, and stage 1's 4 s restarts.
  - Off the map (Dormant, unbuilt cell, infinite W) is stage 0 at once. `DebugPlace` clears the stage and its timers.
  - `WarnRunSeconds` is the unbroken stage ≥ 1 run (kept through 2 → 1), for the G3 gate; `WarnStage2RunSeconds` is the current stage 2 run.
  - Tests: the WarnStages section of the interaction tests, through `DebugWarnSample` and through the tick. The field rebuilds when the player cell, built-chunk topology, or a door/window opening state changes. The Relay warning's bursts on the player's room (`LampFx.Warn` callers), `TargetAcquired` and the ink's wayfinding remain later slices.
